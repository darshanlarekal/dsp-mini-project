\subsection{Quantitative Benchmark}
Table~\ref{tab:benchmark} reports the three detection rules on the same
Kurtosis-guided SES, so that the comparison isolates the thresholding stage.
Spectral SNR gain is defined as the ratio of the mean comb peak to the median
out-of-comb floor in dB, the detection rate is the fraction of segments in
which all three harmonics are declared present, and the false-alarm rate is
the fraction of out-of-comb bins that exceed the threshold. The static rule
is expected to trade false alarms at heavy load against missed detections at
light load, CA-CFAR to lose detections through target masking, and the
proposed rule to hold a near-constant false-alarm rate across the load sweep.
