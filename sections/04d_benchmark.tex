\emph{1) Static threshold.} The fixed level is calibrated once on the $0$~HP
reference at $T_{\text{static}}=0.00228$, the largest Median--MAD level in that
segment's three BPFO harmonic windows, and then held fixed. Even so it has no
stable operating point: at $0$~HP one of the five segments crosses $82\%$ of the
non-harmonic bins while the other four stay near $1\%$, and the detection rate
moves from $0$ to $100\%$ segment by segment, giving a mean of $53.3\%$ with a
$50.6$-point standard deviation; at $1$~HP the mean falls to $40.0\%$ and at
$2$ and $3$~HP it rises to $80.0\%$. The spreads, up to $55$ points, come from a
level that sits on the edge of the SES amplitudes, so whether a harmonic clears
it depends on the segment, not on the load.

\emph{2) CA-CFAR.} Cell averaging follows the load-dependent floor with a
false-alarm share between $5.7\%$ and $7.9\%$ at every load, and detects the
full BPFO comb in every segment, so the mean TDR is $100\%$ at all four loads
with zero spread. Its reference window inherits energy from the fault lines
themselves, which keeps the FAR from falling below about $5.7\%$.

\emph{3) Median--MAD.} The proposed rule also returns $100\%$ at every load
while its false-alarm share stays below CA-CFAR: $6.6$ against $7.0\%$ at
$0$~HP, $6.9$ against $7.9\%$ at $1$~HP, $4.9$ against $5.7\%$ at $2$~HP and
$4.7$ against $6.1\%$ at $3$~HP, so the operating point is not paid for in
extra crossings.

\emph{4) Full-benchmark behaviour.} Across all $36$ CWRU fault records
(inner-race, ball and outer-race defects at $7$, $14$ and $21$~mil, four loads
each), the mean TDR is $57.8\%$ for CA-CFAR and $56.3\%$ for Median--MAD,
against $40.9\%$ for the fixed rule; the corresponding mean FAR is $5.0\%$,
$4.4\%$ and $10.7\%$. On the $14$ MFPT variable-load fault records both adaptive
rules detect the full comb ($100\%$ TDR) with FAR $2.1$--$2.2\%$ (CA-CFAR) and
$1.7$--$1.8\%$ (Median--MAD), while
the CWRU-calibrated fixed level floods the MFPT SES ($88.9\%$ and $99.6\%$
crossings): a threshold calibrated in one dataset's units does not transfer to
another. The healthy baselines show the opposite side of the same comparison:
on the four normal CWRU records the fixed threshold crosses no eligible bin
($0\%$), CA-CFAR $1.9\%$ and Median--MAD $1.0\%$; on the MFPT baseline
recordings CA-CFAR crosses $0.1\%$ and Median--MAD $0.0\%$, while the fixed
level floods $91.7\%$ of the bins.

\emph{5) SNR gain.} The filter and envelope stages add $13.4$--$16.4$~dB of
spectral SNR on the outer-race record, most at $1$~HP, where the raw record
sits at $+5.29$~dB and the SES reaches $+21.71$~dB. On the MFPT outer-race
records the chain adds $16.1$~dB, while the MFPT inner-race records already
carry sharp raw harmonics so the gain is essentially zero ($-0.25$~dB). The
detector choice does not affect this column: all three rules read the same
SES.
