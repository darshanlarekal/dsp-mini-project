\subsection{Evaluation Metrics}
The evaluation uses the CWRU $2$~HP Reliance Electric rig with a dynamometer
load and the 6205-2RS drive-end bearing, sampled at $12$~kHz over the four load
steps of Table~I, with outer-race spalls of $7$, $14$ and $21$~mil. The MFPT
records add seven radial load levels from $25$ to $300$~lbs. The kinematic
frequencies of Table~I, $f_{\text{BPFO}}=107.36$~Hz and
$f_{\text{BPFI}}=162.19$~Hz at no load, serve as ground truth.

Four figures of merit are computed on the SES. All are evaluated across the
motor loads and fault severities listed in Section~III, and the reported values
are means over $60$ Monte Carlo iterations.

\emph{1) Spectral SNR improvement.} The ratio of the harmonic energy to the
local noise floor is
\begin{equation}
  \text{SNR}_{\text{SES}} = 10\log_{10}
  \frac{\sum_{m=1}^{M}\left|\text{SES}[k_m]\right|^{2}}
  {\frac{1}{\left|\mathcal{K}_{\text{noise}}\right|}
  \sum_{k\in\mathcal{K}_{\text{noise}}}\left|\text{SES}[k]\right|^{2}},
  \label{eq:snrses}
\end{equation}
where $k_m$ runs over the bins inside the $\pm1.5\%$ slip window
$\mathcal{B}_m$ around each harmonic $m f_{\text{fault}}$, and
$\mathcal{K}_{\text{noise}}$ collects the non-harmonic bins of the
$10$--$400$~Hz demodulation band. The gain produced by the filtering and
envelope stages is the difference
$\Delta\text{SNR}=\text{SNR}_{\text{SES}}-\text{SNR}_{\text{raw}}$.

\emph{2) True detection rate.} The fraction of the targeted harmonics
($1\times$, $2\times$, $3\times f_{\text{fault}}$) that clear the threshold is
\begin{equation}
  \text{TDR} = \frac{1}{M}\sum_{m=1}^{M}
  \mathbb{I}\!\left(\max_{k\in\mathcal{B}_m}\text{SES}[k] > T[k]\right)\times100\%,
  \label{eq:tdr}
\end{equation}
with $\mathbb{I}(\cdot)$ the indicator function and
$\mathcal{B}_m=[m f_{\text{fault}}(1-0.015),\,m f_{\text{fault}}(1+0.015)]$ the
slip search boundary.

\emph{3) False-alarm rate.} The number of bins between $15$ and $400$~Hz,
outside the harmonic masks, that still breach $T[k]$. It is reported as an
absolute count rather than a fraction, because a threshold that is slightly too
low floods the band and the bin count shows it more clearly than a percentage.

\emph{4) Execution latency.} The wall-clock time for one $2.0$~s frame
($N=24\,000$ samples at $12$~kHz), measured on the same machine for every
configuration.
