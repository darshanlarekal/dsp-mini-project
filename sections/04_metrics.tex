\subsection{Evaluation Metrics}
Four measures are computed on the SES: spectral SNR, true detection rate,
false-alarm rate and execution latency. Each is scored on every record at every
load and defect size listed in Section~III. Every CWRU record is split into
five non-overlapping $2.0$~s segments ($N=24\,000$ samples at $12$~kHz), and
each segment is processed independently; a reported number is the mean over
those segments, with the sample standard deviation where stated. The MFPT
records last $3$~s, so each contributes one $2.0$~s segment.

The data come from the CWRU $2$~HP Reliance Electric rig with a dynamometer
load and the 6205-2RS drive-end bearing, sampled at $12$~kHz over the four load
steps of Table~I, with outer-race spalls of $7$, $14$ and $21$~mil. The MFPT
records add seven radial load levels from $25$ to $300$~lbs. The fault
frequencies of Table~I, $f_{\text{BPFO}}=107.36$~Hz and
$f_{\text{BPFI}}=162.19$~Hz at no load, serve as ground truth.

\emph{1) Spectral SNR.} The first measure sets the energy at the harmonics
against the local noise floor,
\begin{equation}
  \text{SNR}_{\text{SES}} = 10\log_{10}
  \frac{\frac{1}{M}\sum_{m=1}^{M}
  \max_{k\in\mathcal{B}_m}\left|\text{SES}[k]\right|^{2}}
  {\frac{1}{\left|\mathcal{K}_{\text{noise}}\right|}
  \sum_{k\in\mathcal{K}_{\text{noise}}}\left|\text{SES}[k]\right|^{2}},
  \label{eq:snrses}
\end{equation}
where $\mathcal{B}_m$ is the $\pm1.5\%$ slip window around harmonic
$m f_{\text{fault}}$, so the numerator is the mean of the three per-harmonic
peak powers, and $\mathcal{K}_{\text{noise}}$ collects the non-harmonic bins
of the evaluation band above $10$~Hz. The raw baseline uses the identical
estimator on $x_0[n]$: a Hann-windowed, zero-padded one-sided FFT amplitude
spectrum cropped to the same band. The difference
$\Delta\text{SNR}=\text{SNR}_{\text{SES}}-\text{SNR}_{\text{raw}}$ is therefore
the gain the filter and envelope stages add, not an artefact of two different
spectral estimators.

\emph{2) True detection rate.} The second measure is the share of the targeted
harmonics ($1\times$, $2\times$ and $3\times f_{\text{fault}}$) that clear the
threshold,
\begin{equation}
  \text{TDR} = \frac{1}{M}\sum_{m=1}^{M}
  \mathbb{I}\!\left(\max_{k\in\mathcal{B}_m}
  \left(\text{SES}[k]-T[k]\right) > 0\right)\times100\%,
  \label{eq:tdr}
\end{equation}
with $\mathbb{I}(\cdot)$ the indicator function and
$\mathcal{B}_m=[m f_{\text{fault}}(1-0.015),\,m f_{\text{fault}}(1+0.015)]$ the
slip search boundary.

\emph{3) False-alarm rate.} The third measure is the share of the eligible
bins above $15$~Hz, outside the harmonic masks, that still cross $T[k]$,
\begin{equation}
  \text{FAR} = \frac{100\%}{\left|\mathcal{K}_{N}\right|}
  \sum_{k\in\mathcal{K}_{N}}\mathbb{I}\!\left(\text{SES}[k]>T[k]\right),
  \label{eq:far}
\end{equation}
where $\mathcal{K}_{N}$ collects the non-harmonic bins above $15$~Hz. A rate
keeps records with different FFT lengths comparable.

\emph{4) Execution latency.} The wall-clock time for one $2.0$~s frame
($N=24\,000$ samples at $12$~kHz), taken on the same machine for every
configuration.
