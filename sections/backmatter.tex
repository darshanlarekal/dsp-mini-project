\section*{Author Contributions}
\textbf{Vidyashankar T} curated the datasets, formulated the fault model and
implemented slip-tolerant frequency tracking. \textbf{Nikunj Mayur Jethawa}
designed the Kurtosis-guided band selection, implemented the FIR and cascade-SOS
IIR filters and analysed poles, zeros and group delay. \textbf{Darshan L Arekal}
developed the squared-envelope demodulator, calibrated the windowed SES,
implemented the CA-CFAR and Median--MAD thresholding, and led the manuscript
integration. \textbf{Sanjay Hariharan} ran the multi-load benchmarking and the
fixed-point feasibility study, and led the manuscript integration. All authors
approved the final manuscript.
